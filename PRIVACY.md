# Privacy policy

Publisher: Adkid-Zephyr. Effective date: 2026-10-07.

Anti-Defensive Writing is a package of writing instructions. It includes no MCP server, hosted backend, analytics, tracking, account system, or publisher-operated storage. The publisher does not receive or retain manuscript text, prompts, files, personal data, or usage telemetry through this plugin. The package makes no network calls and sends no data to external recipients.

Text and files you provide to Codex or ChatGPT are processed by that host under its privacy policy and your account settings. This policy does not change the host's data processing or retention. You control which text and files to provide and may uninstall the plugin through the host.

If you voluntarily post an issue on GitHub, its content is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not include confidential manuscripts or personal data in public issues. Contact the publisher through https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/anti-defensive-writing-Skill/issues.
